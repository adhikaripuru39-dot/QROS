# The QROS Bill of Rights

*Ratified Founding Governance document. Faithful transcription.*

**Preamble** — Every research project, dataset, algorithm, software component, and contributor within QROS deserves an environment that promotes fairness, transparency, scientific integrity, and engineering excellence. The following rights are guaranteed by the platform and shall not be violated without documented justification.

### Article 1 — Right to Reproducibility
Every experiment has the right to be reproduced. Every experiment must include:
1. Complete dataset references
2. Software version
3. Configuration
4. Parameters
5. Random seeds (when applicable)
6. Execution environment
7. Date and time
8. Author
9. Version identifier

If another researcher cannot reproduce an experiment, the experiment is considered incomplete until the discrepancy is understood.

### Article 2 — Right to Traceability
Every important decision shall be traceable. Questions such as:
- Why was this strategy approved?
- Why was this parameter changed?
- Why was this model rejected?

...must always have documented answers. Institutional knowledge must never depend solely on memory.

### Article 3 — Right to Honest Evaluation
No strategy shall receive special treatment. All strategies shall be evaluated using the same standards. Evaluation criteria shall be documented before testing begins. This reduces unconscious bias and ensures fair comparison.

### Article 4 — Right to Failure
Failure is recognized as a valuable research outcome. Failed experiments shall be documented rather than hidden. A failed hypothesis that improves understanding is considered productive research.

### Article 5 — Right to Documentation
Every major component has the right to complete documentation, including: Purpose, Assumptions, Inputs, Outputs, Limitations, Dependencies, Validation results. Documentation is part of the deliverable, not optional work.

### Article 6 — Right to Explainability
Every production decision should be explainable. While complex models may not be fully interpretable, their behavior, assumptions, and limitations must be documented as clearly as possible.

### Article 7 — Right to Risk Protection
No strategy has the authority to override established risk controls. Risk management policies apply consistently across the platform.

### Article 8 — Right to Continuous Improvement
Every approved strategy remains open to review and improvement. Approval is not permanent. Continued performance and evidence determine whether a strategy remains in production.
