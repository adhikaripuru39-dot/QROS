# QROS - Stage 7 Research Pipeline Charter

**Status:** Governance-only · **Scope:** One page · **Authority:** QROS Constitution (Draft for Ratification), Founding Governance Documents, Master Handbook Parts 1-7, Stage 1-6 packages, Project State reference-only note.

> **Ratification note.** The Constitution remains draft governance. Stage 7 makes no claim of ratification, production readiness, or approval. Reference-only folders (`.claude/`, `_extracted/`, `scratch/`, `trading_engine/`) are read-only context, not governance baseline.

## 1. Purpose
Define the **linear research workflow** that turns approved hypotheses and approved features into **governed research work**, with explicit gates and no shortcuts. Stage 7 fixes the stage order, mandatory gates and sign-offs, failure/rollback handling, research boundaries, and allowed artifacts. It builds no pipeline, feature, hypothesis, strategy, backtest, or execution code.

## 2. Governing authority
- Constitution - Articles IV, V, VI, IX (evidence; data precedes conclusions; scientific lifecycle; traceability).
- Founding Governance - Bill of Rights (Traceability, Honest Evaluation, Documentation); Code of Scientific Conduct.
- Master Handbook - Part 7 (Research Pipeline, 15-stage workflow) as primary; Parts 1-6 (data, quality, features, hypotheses).
- Stage 1-6 governance packages; Project State reference-only note.

## 3. What the research pipeline is in QROS
The **linear, gatekeeper-controlled 15-stage workflow** (Part 7 §1) that converts an approved, registered hypothesis and approved features into governed research. It advances **only** through formal review and sign-off, with **zero quality-gate violations and no shortcuts**. No strategy is built outside this pipeline; no backtest runs beyond governed research; no execution occurs at any stage.

## 4. Allowed work (Stage 7)
- Author the pipeline-rule map (stage order, approvals/sign-offs, gate failures & rollback, research execution boundaries, allowed artifacts, progression blockers).
- Specify the mandatory stage gates and the failure-handling protocol.
- Produce governance/specification documents only.

## 5. Prohibited work (Stage 7)
- **No** research pipeline, feature, hypothesis, strategy, backtest, or execution code.
- **No** live trading, order dispatch, or execution code.
- **No** strategy development outside the governed pipeline.
- **No** backtesting beyond governed research.
- **No** claim of ratification, production readiness, or approval.

## 6. Entry criteria
- Stages 1-6 packages committed and accepted; Project State reference-only note in effect.
- Constitution treated as draft.
- Scope limited to research-pipeline **specification** (Parts 1-7).

## 7. Exit criteria
- Pipeline map, charter, checklist, and scope documented, consistent, and approved.
- Every gate, sign-off, and failure rule traceable to a governing source.
- No prohibited work performed; no implementation artifacts.
- Artifacts for Stage 8 entry specified (see `STAGE_7_SCOPE.md` §Outputs).

## 8. Do-not-proceed (blocking)
Before the Stage 7 pipeline rules are approved, the following are forbidden and halt work:
1. **No** strategy implementation, backtesting beyond governed research, or execution of any kind.
2. **No** stage advancement without its mandatory gate passing and sign-off.
3. **No** relaxing a quality gate or skipping a stage ("no shortcuts").
4. **No** ratification / approval / production-readiness claim.

See `STAGE_7_CHECKLIST.md` gate **G0** for the operational blocking gate.
