Chapter 2: Quality Gates &amp; Failure Protocols
[QUALITY GATE FAILURE RULE] IF ANY STAGE VIOLATES QUALITY GATE CRITERIA:  ├── The project is immediately halted.  ├── The reason for failure is documented in the Experiment Registry.  └── The hypothesis is officially marked REJECTED or returned to Stage 3 for theoretical re-formulation. Retroactive threshold relaxation is strictly prohibited.
