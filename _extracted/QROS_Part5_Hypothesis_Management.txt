QROS INSTITUTIONAL QUANTITATIVE RESEARCH FRAMEWORK
PART 5: RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS MANAGEMENTHypothesis Registration, Economic Rationale &amp; Institutional Specifications
[HYPOTHESIS MANAGEMENT MANDATE] Quantitative research without a prior economic hypothesis is data mining. Every research project must submit a formal, immutable Hypothesis Document before analyzing backtest results.
Chapter 1: Institutional Hypothesis Architecture &amp; Mandatory Template
All alpha research projects must formally register a 22-field Hypothesis Document into the institutional Research Registry.
Field ID
Field Name
Institutional Requirement / Specification
HYP-01
Hypothesis ID
Globally unique identifier (e.g., HYP-EQUITY-MICROSTRUCTURE-2026-0042).
HYP-02
Research Question
Precise scientific question (e.g., Does high L2 order book imbalance predict 5-minute forward returns?).
HYP-03
Market Logic
Microstructural mechanism or market mechanics driving the phenomenon.
HYP-04
Economic Theory
Underlying economic equilibrium or risk compensation theory.
HYP-05
Behavioral Theory
Cognitive bias, loss aversion, or institutional constraint causing market mispricing.
HYP-06
Expected Edge
Quantified expected edge capacity, direction, and decay half-life.
HYP-07
Market Conditions
Specific market regimes (e.g., High Volatility, Elevated Spread) where edge manifests.
HYP-08
Assumptions
Core assumptions regarding liquidity, execution costs, and market impact.
HYP-09
Independent Variables
Primary predictive features (e.g., Order Flow Imbalance, VPIN).
HYP-10
Dependent Variables
Target return metric (e.g., 5-minute mid-price forward return).
HYP-11
Control Variables
Confounding market factors held constant (e.g., Sector Return, Market Beta).
HYP-12
Expected Direction
Theoretical sign of the relationship (Positive / Negative correlation).
HYP-13
Supporting Literature
Academic papers or empirical industry research validating the mechanism.
HYP-14
Contradicting Literature
Academic arguments against the persistence of the anomaly.
HYP-15
Alternative Explanations
Competing hypotheses (e.g., risk premium vs behavioral mispricing vs noise).
HYP-16
Data Requirements
Required dataset snapshot versions, depth levels, and sample frequency.
HYP-17
Testing Requirements
Statistical tests (e.g., t-stat, Deflated Sharpe Ratio, Walk-Forward CV).
HYP-18
Acceptance Criteria
Strict thresholds (e.g., OOS Sharpe Ratio &gt; 2.0, t-stat &gt; 3.0, DSR p-value &lt; 0.01).
HYP-19
Rejection Criteria
Conditions triggering immediate hypothesis rejection (e.g., t-stat &lt; 2.0).
HYP-20
Confidence Level
Required statistical significance level (e.g., 99.5% confidence).
HYP-21
Replication Requirements
Independent replication by a secondary Quant Researcher.
HYP-22
Retirement Criteria
Performance decay triggers for retiring a live production alpha factor.
Chapter 2: Governance &amp; Hypothesis Approval Workflow
• Pre-Testing Approval: Quant Researcher submits HYP document; Head of Research and Lead Statistician must approve before code execution.
• Immutable Versioning: Hypothesis text is locked upon submission (SHA-256 registered). Modifying hypotheses post-test to fit data is grounds for immediate termination.
