# The QROS Bill of Responsibilities

**Ratified Founding Governing. Faithful transcription.** Rights create trust; responsibilities create accountability. Every participant and every subsystem shares responsibility for maintaining the quality of QROS.

### Responsibility 1 — Research honestly
Do not manipulate data to support conclusions.

### Responsibility 2 — Document every important decision
Future contributors should understand not only what changed but also why.

### Responsibility 3 — Protect trading capital
Capital preservation takes precedence over maximizing returns.

### Responsibility 4 — Question assumptions
Every assumption should be open to challenge when supported by evidence.

### Responsibility 5 — Maintain code quality
Code should be readable, testable, and maintainable.

### Responsibility 6 — Respect statistical evidence
Do not accept conclusions based solely on intuition, isolated examples, or cherry-picked results.

### Responsibility 7 — Preserve institutional knowledge
Research findings, failures, and lessons learned should be recorded to strengthen the platform over time.

### Responsibility 8 — Support continuous learning
Every completed project should contribute knowledge back into QROS.
