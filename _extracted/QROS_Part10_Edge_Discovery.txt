Chapter 2: Real Edge vs. False Edge Audit Framework
To prevent approving curve-fitted strategies, every candidate signal is evaluated against 5 False Edge diagnostics:
• 1. Curve-Fitting / Overfitting: Detected when performance drops drastically under slight parameter perturbations (+/- 10%).
• 2. Data Mining / Snooping: Detected when DSR (Deflated Sharpe Ratio) fails after adjusting for total tested trial count.
• 3. Coincidence / Luck: Detected when Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) is statistically indistinguishable from zero under Permutation Testing.
• 4. Execution Mirage: Edge disappears once Level 2 order book spread costs and square-root market impact are subtracted.
• 5. Capacity Bottleneck: Edge exists only for trivial trade sizes (&lt; $10,000) and cannot scale to institutional capital ($50M+).
