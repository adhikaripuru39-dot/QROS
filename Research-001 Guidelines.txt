Mission Objective
Establish a rigorous, reproducible, auditable Edge Discovery and Edge Validation framework for QROS.
The purpose of this mission is NOT to discover a profitable strategy immediately.
The purpose is to build the scientific and engineering infrastructure through which QROS can:
observe market behavior,
formulate hypotheses,
test hypotheses,
reject false hypotheses,
identify promising effects,
validate edge candidates,
test robustness,
promote only sufficiently validated candidates to confirmed edges.
1. NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLE
QROS must never manufacture an edge.
The system must be capable of concluding:
NO EDGE FOUND

when the evidence does not support an edge.
Do not relax standards because a research search produces poor results.
Do not modify acceptance thresholds simply to produce a positive result.
Do not optimize until a result becomes profitable merely for the purpose of obtaining an edge.
Do not label a profitable backtest as a confirmed edge without passing the complete validation process.

5. DEFINE "MARKET EDGE"
Create:
01_EDGE_DEFINITION.md
QROS should define an edge as a repeatable conditional statistical/economic advantage that survives appropriate validation and realistic trading costs.
The definition must distinguish:
Observation
Something appears to happen.
Pattern
A recurring descriptive relationship.
Hypothesis
A formally testable proposition.
Effect
A measurable statistical relationship.
Edge Candidate
An effect showing sufficient initial evidence to justify deeper validation.
Confirmed Edge
An effect satisfying all QROS validation requirements.
Strategy
A complete trading methodology constructed around one or more validated edges.
These terms must never be used interchangeably.

8. HYPOTHESIS QUALITY STANDARD
A hypothesis must be:
Specific
Not:
"Gold tends to reverse."

Instead:
A precisely defined condition produces a measurable directional distribution under specified market conditions.

Falsifiable
There must be a result that can prove it wrong.
Mechanically testable
Another researcher should be able to reproduce the test.
Time-bounded
Define when the behavior is expected to occur.
Condition-bounded
Define the market conditions.
Quantifiable
The expected effect must be measurable.

This is a critical requirement.
QROS research must not simply generate:

12. DATA LEAKAGE PREVENTION
Create a dedicated section for leakage prevention.
The system must detect/prevent:
future information entering features,
future bars influencing signals,
look-ahead bias,
improperly aligned timestamps,
survivorship bias,
selection bias,
training/test contamination,
normalization using future information,
parameter selection using the final test set.
If there is uncertainty, the conservative interpretation must be used.
13. DATA SPLITTING
QROS research should distinguish:
Research / Discovery
        ↓
Development
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
Final Out-of-Sample Test
The final test set must remain protected from iterative optimization.
The system must record when data has been used for hypothesis development.
14. TRANSACTION COSTS
Every trading hypothesis must eventually be tested under realistic assumptions.
At minimum consider:

exit price assumptions
Do not permit:

15. STATISTICAL VALIDATION
Create:
05_STATISTICAL_VALIDATION_STANDARD.md
The framework should consider:

Do not rely exclusively on p-values.
A statistically significant but economically trivial effect is not automatically an edge.
16. MULTIPLE TESTING
This is mandatory.
The system must track:
Total hypotheses generated
Total hypotheses tested
Total feature combinations
Total parameter combinations
Total rejected hypotheses
Total promising hypotheses
Total candidates
Total confirmed edges
Research results must retain enough metadata to identify the degree of experimentation that produced them.
QROS must not hide unsuccessful experiments.
17. EDGE CANDIDATE STANDARD
Create:
06_EDGE_CANDIDATE_STANDARD.md
A candidate should only be promoted when it demonstrates sufficient initial evidence.
The candidate must have:
clearly defined conditions,
reproducible methodology,
sufficient observations,
positive effect after costs where applicable,
plausible economic mechanism,
acceptable statistical evidence,
no known leakage,
documented research history.
Candidate ≠ confirmed edge.
18. ROBUSTNESS VALIDATION
Create:
07_ROBUSTNESS_STANDARD.md
A candidate must eventually be tested against reasonable variations.
Examples:
Time variation
Does the effect exist across different periods?
Regime variation
Does it survive different volatility/trend environments?
Parameter variation
Does a tiny parameter change destroy the effect?
Cost variation
Does increased slippage eliminate the result?
Market variation
Where theoretically appropriate, does related-market behavior support the mechanism?
Walk-forward
Does the relationship persist when tested sequentially through time?
Monte Carlo / resampling
Where appropriate, determine whether observed performance could plausibly arise from randomness.

21. EDGE INVALIDATION
This is essential.
Every confirmed edge must have conditions under which it becomes invalid.
Examples:
Expected effect disappears
Expected value falls below threshold
Market regime changes
Transaction costs become prohibitive
OOS degradation exceeds tolerance
Structural mechanism is invalidated
A confirmed edge is therefore not immortal.
22. EDGE → MODULE RULE
Document this explicitly.
Confirmed Edge
      ↓
Edge Specification
      ↓
Edge Module
But:
One edge does not automatically equal one complete trading strategy.

A strategy may combine multiple validated edges.
Similarly:
One engine may contain multiple modules.

Maintain separation between:
Edge
Economic/statistical advantage.
Module
Software implementation.
Strategy
Trading decision architecture.
Engine
Functional software subsystem.
Portfolio
Combination and allocation of strategies.
